Privacy & terms

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No accounts, no sign-up and no forms, so we never receive your name, email or anything you type.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only in your own browser, and you can delete them at any time.

What is stored, and where

To remember your progress, the site uses your browser's local storage, a small storage area on your own device. It holds:

  • which words you have practised and how many times you spelled each one correctly or incorrectly;
  • your settings (word list, topic, level, voice, speed, mistakes allowed and similar);
  • a note that you have seen the privacy notice, so it is not shown again.

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. Local storage is not a cookie: it is not sent with web requests.

To delete it: open Settings and choose Reset all, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Using a private or incognito window means nothing is kept after you close it.

Things that are not under our control

  • Hosting. Like every website, the server that delivers these pages (our hosting provider, Vercel) automatically receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to send you the page, and may keep short-term logs for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or track visitors.
  • Speech. Words are read aloud by your browser's or device's own text-to-speech voices. Some browsers offer online voices (for example, voices marked "Google" or "Online"); with those, your browser sends the word being spoken to that company to produce the sound. You can choose a different voice in Settings.
  • PDF export. The site loads no third-party files when you open it. Only if you press Export PDF, it downloads two open-source libraries (jsPDF and jsPDF-AutoTable) from the cdnjs network run by Cloudflare, which receives your IP address as part of delivering the files. The PDF itself is created on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.
  • External links. Links to other websites (such as Wiktionary) open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The site collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so it can be used without sharing any details.

Word data and licences

Definitions are adapted from Wiktionary (CC BY-SA), via the Free Dictionary API, and WordNet (WordNet licence), with many written or rewritten for learners. CEFR levels come from the CEFR-J and Octanove vocabulary profiles (CC BY-SA 4.0). Word frequencies come from wordfreq. The word list was put together using published IELTS listening vocabulary and the Academic Word List as guides. Because the sources are share-alike, the word data on this site is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Changes
